# Nexus-CORE trademarks

The code is free software under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 (see `LICENSE`). The GPL covers the code. It does not cover
the name or the logo; this page does.

The point is simple: anyone may change Nexus-CORE and share what they
made, but only the real thing may be called Nexus-CORE. Somebody who
downloads "Nexus-CORE" should get Nexus-CORE, not somebody else's
build of it.

## The marks

- The name **Nexus-CORE**, and **Nexus** when used for this project.
- The **node-graph N** logo, in any colour or size.
- The artwork in `image/artwork/`, which carries the logo.

All belong to DAVIDOSDAS.

## Allowed without asking

- **Sharing unmodified copies** of an official release -- the ISO, the
  image, the source -- under its own name. The GPL already allows
  this, and mirrors help people get it.
- **Talking about it:** reviews, articles, videos, tutorials, and
  saying that something works with Nexus-CORE or is based on it.
- **Community use:** a user group, a forum, a wiki about Nexus-CORE,
  as long as it does not look official.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Calling a changed version Nexus-CORE**, or anything confusingly
  close. "Based on Nexus-CORE" is fine; "Nexus-CORE Gaming Edition"
  made by someone else is not.
- **Using the logo or the artwork on a changed version**, on a product,
  or in a way that suggests DAVIDOSDAS made or endorses it.
- **Selling anything under the name**, or putting it in a domain name,
  app store listing or account name that could be mistaken for the
  project's own.

## If you make your own version

Rename it and remove the marks. In practice:

1. Change `NAME` and `PRETTY_NAME` in `image/identity.sh`.
2. Remove or replace everything in `image/artwork/`.
3. Push the image under your own registry and name, not
   `davidosdas-official`.

Everything else -- the code, the profiles, the tools -- is yours to use
under the GPL.

Open an issue or contact DAVIDOSDAS. Permission is given in writing
and only for what it names.
